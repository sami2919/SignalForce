# Lessons

Rules learned from corrections. Read at session start.

## 2026-11-19 — Existing customers are not "warm intros"

- **Correction:** the Magic/Loyalist demo listed existing Loyalist customers as "warm intro /
  expansion" accounts in the report and on event meeting lists. The user: they are already
  customers, so they can't be there.
- **Rule:** an account that already buys the product is excluded from every outreach surface
  (prospect ranking, meeting lists, drafts, contacts, Clay). Do not re-frame it as a softer kind
  of target. If customers are shown at all, it is only to let a human check the detection, and
  the heading says they are excluded.
- **Check before shipping a list:** for every output a seller acts on, ask "could anyone on this
  list already be a customer?" and pin the answer with a test.

## 2026-11-20 — The account is the hospitality group, not the restaurant

- **Correction:** I reviewed the demo report as if reps would contact individual restaurants
  ("turn legal names into restaurant cards"). The user: Magic sells to hospitality groups.
  Loyalist's customers are groups (Major Food Group, Dinex, Hogsalt, USHG), not venues.
- **Rule:** the unit of account is the operating group. Venue signals are evidence about a
  group; a venue opening means "this group is expanding". Rank, present and contact at group
  level (founder, CEO, VP or director of operations, head of guest experience or marketing),
  and treat a prospect with no known parent group as unresolved, not as a single-venue account.
- **Check:** before judging a list, ask "what does the buyer look like?" and confirm the unit
  of the list matches it.
